The problem

"Where is that blazer from?" is still a research project.

Viewers want what characters wear at the moment they see it. Hong Hae-in's wardrobe in Queen of Tears filled fan sites for months, and each of those posts started with someone leaving the show to go searching.

Today

  1. Pause the episode.
  2. Screenshot the scene.
  3. Search "Hae-in red blazer episode 7".
  4. Scroll a fan site for someone who already identified it.
  5. Find out it costs $2,595, with no cheaper option in sight.

Minutes of searching on another device. Often no answer.

With Mina

Tap the blazer.

One step, on the screen you are already watching, with the exact piece and three cheaper ones.

Hypothesis to validate

Hypothesis to validate

These are the three numbers the pilot is designed to measure. They are targets, not results.
