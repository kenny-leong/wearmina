How it works

How Mina knows what is on screen

Four steps run between the frame you are watching and the product page. Select a step to see what is behind it.

Platforms

One wardrobe graph, three screens

The same data powers every surface. Each card switches the demo above to that mode.

Browser extension

Tags sit on top of the player in Chrome, Safari and Edge. The first product to ship, with no deal needed from any streamer.

Smart TV + phone

The TV shows a quiet prompt. Your phone pairs with one scan and fills with the looks from the scene, live.

Mobile

For viewers who watch on a phone, looks slide up in a sheet under the video. One thumb, no app switching.

Business model

Three ways Mina makes money

Free for viewers. Brands pay when a scene sells something, and for knowing which scenes do.

Per sale

Affiliate commission

Every tag carries an attribution code. The brand pays a share of each order that started on screen.

Per placement

Featured looks

When the original piece is custom or sold out, a brand can sponsor the closest-match slot. Always labeled as featured.

Per seat

Insights subscription

Brands and studios see which scenes, characters and items create demand, episode by episode.
